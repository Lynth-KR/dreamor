dreamor is a header-only C++20 modeling layer for optimization models: index sets as relations over your data,
variable families over a set, and sums over sets and their groups, on HiGHS, CPLEX Concert and OR-Tools MathOpt.

- **[Quick start](https://github.com/Lynth-KR/dreamor#quick-start)** - one model, whole, on HiGHS, in the README.
- **[Guide](guide/index.md)** - how to do each thing a model does, a page a topic.
- **[API reference](reference/index.md)** - every public type and function.
- **[Philosophy](philosophy.md)** - what dreamor holds to and why, and what it leaves to you.
- **Agents** writing dreamor models get the skill in
  [`skills/dreamor/`](https://github.com/Lynth-KR/dreamor/tree/main/skills/dreamor): this documentation as files,
  and the rules they most often break.
